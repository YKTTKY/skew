# Coverage only — no trade recommendations

Skew surfaces analyzed coverage (Stories, Bias, Sentiment, Rationales, Price Context) for **Pre-Trade Research** and **does not** issue buy, sell, hold, or equivalent trade recommendations (including soft “should act” signals). This is a deliberate product and liability boundary: alternatives would reclassify the product toward advice/alpha and change regulatory and UX expectations. UI copy and AI prompts must not drift into recommendations.
